# Provenance and attribution

This archive mixes material with three different owners. That distinction matters
before anything here is reproduced in a published book, so it is recorded up
front rather than sorted out later.

## 1. Source code excerpts — `code-samples/`

Excerpts from **`anchor-os/custom-biome-lint`**, a public repository authored by
the repository owner with AI assistance (45 of its 63 commits carry a
machine-readable AI co-author trailer — see
`sample-pr-evidence/git-authorship-evidence.md`).

- Governed by **that repository's own licence.** Check it and state it in the
  book's front matter before reproducing any excerpt.
- All excerpts are short and annotated. Full files are not reproduced here.
- Pulled at package version **0.4.5**, 2026-09-07. Re-verify against a frozen
  edition SHA before print.

## 2. Automated review output — `sample-pr-evidence/pr-08-*`, `pr-12-*`

Verbatim review bodies produced by **CodeRabbit** (`coderabbitai[bot]`) on the
repository owner's own public pull requests.

- These reviews are **already publicly visible** on the PRs they were posted to.
  Reproducing them here discloses nothing new.
- They are CodeRabbit's output, not the author's writing. Attribute them as such
  wherever they are quoted — in the book, name the tool and link the PR.
- **Quote specific findings, do not reproduce whole reviews in print.** A finding
  tied to a real bug is fair use and is the point; a wholesale reprint of a
  commercial tool's output as book content is a different thing.
- Do not generalise about the product. Every claim about the reviewer stays tied
  to one quotable, verifiable finding — it was right about a great deal here, and
  demonstrably wrong twice, and both belong in the book.

## 3. Pull request descriptions and editorial notes — everything else
